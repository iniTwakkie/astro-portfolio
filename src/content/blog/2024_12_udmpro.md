Most home WiFi routers are fine until you start having issues: slow speeds, devices dropping off, no idea what's actually on your network.

The UDM Pro fixes that. It's effectively the control centre for your internet and home network. You can:

- keep your internet stable and fast
- see every device connected
- block unwanted access and improve security
- manage your WiFi properly if you add UniFi access points

A bonus: if you use UniFi cameras, it records footage to the hard drive inside the unit, so you don't pay for cloud storage. Live view, replays, and motion alerts all in one app.

If you want a strong, reliable network that's easy to manage, the UDM Pro is hard to beat for the money.
