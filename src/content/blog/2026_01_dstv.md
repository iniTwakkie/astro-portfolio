If you've tried to stream DStv outside South Africa, you've hit the same wall as everyone else: the app won't play, or it buffers, or it says the content isn't available in your region. You're paying for the subscription, but you can't reliably watch it.

The setup that's worked for me is a **Tailscale exit node in South Africa**, so DStv thinks you're back home.

## How it works

Tailscale is a private VPN that connects your own devices. Instead of trusting a random VPN provider, you use a machine you control (at home or a VPS) as your gateway. That machine is your **exit node**.

Mark it as an exit node in the Tailscale admin console, then on your laptop or phone pick *Use Exit Node* and select it. All your traffic now routes through South Africa:

- your stream comes from a South African IP
- the route feels local to DStv
- it's far more stable than most public VPNs

## Why this beats a public VPN

Public VPNs often fail at streaming because their IPs get flagged, speeds are inconsistent, and they're overloaded on big match days. Your own exit node is your IP, your connection, and it's consistent.

## Set it up

1. Put a node in South Africa — a home PC, Raspberry Pi, or a small SA VPS.
2. Install Tailscale and connect it to your tailnet.
3. Enable **Exit Node** on that device.
4. From anywhere, open Tailscale and choose *Use Exit Node* → your South African node.

Then open DStv and hit play.

## Bonus

Once the node exists you can also use it for SA-only sites, banking that hates international logins, and secure access to your home network.

One thing to be clear about: I pay for a valid DStv subscription and use this to access content I legitimately subscribe to while traveling. Check DStv's terms and use it responsibly.
